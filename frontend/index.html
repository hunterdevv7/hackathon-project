The turn

Quantum randomness

Don’t hide the seed. Remove it.

A measurement isn’t computed. Its outcome doesn’t exist until it happens, so there’s no seed to steal.

Classical

output = f(seed)

Hide the seed better and the attack gets harder, never impossible.

Quantum

outcome = measure(H|0⟩)

A Hadamard gate makes an equal mix of 0 and 1. Measuring it gives a bit nobody could know in advance.

“A qubit can be 0 and 1 at once. Measure it and it picks a side, undetermined until that exact moment.”
